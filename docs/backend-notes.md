## Story locking and admin-only edits

- **Stories are only for those who collected the card (user's rule, 2026-10-16):** for anyone who hasn't checked in there (and didn't add the card, `addedBy`), `GET /api/locations/:slug` cuts `description` to the preview on the server (`storyTeaser` in `locationController.js`: the first paragraph, or two when the story has more than 3 and the EN opening is 20 words or fewer) and sets `storyLocked: true`; the detail views show that as is and refetch the full story after a check-in. `PUT` refuses a `description` change (403) from such a user, and on a `storyLocked` card the Edit form hides the story fields and leaves `description` out of the save, and since 2026-11-08 the Edit button, `PUT /:slug`, `POST /:slug/cover` and `DELETE /:slug` are for the admin account only (`utils/admin.js`, `adminOnly` middleware, `isAdmin` on the public user). On History, landmarks are shown as `unlocked` for display, so `LocationCard` gets `holdable={landmark.unlocked}` to keep the showcase (long press / double click, which shows the full story) to cards the user really collected.

## Database round trips and the in-memory card catalog

- **Database round trips (2026-11-13):** until the move to Frankfurt each query to Atlas cost ~106 ms from the dev machine and ~100 ms from Render (now ~8 ms and ~1–2 ms), so a handler that awaits its queries one by one is slow however light they are (the profile's endpoints were ~20 in a row, 2 s). Start independent queries together (`Promise.all`, as in `buildProgress`, `buildAchievementStats`, `syncPacks`, `getAchievements`), reuse a list already loaded instead of querying it again, and give big finds and their `populate` `batchSize(BIG_BATCH)` (`utils/dbBatch.js`), since every batch after the first 101 documents is another round trip. **Cards come from memory:** `services/locationCatalog.js` keeps every card without its story (`CATALOG_FIELDS`) plus the totals the profile and achievements need (city, landmark, per district and quarter, legends, embassies), checked on each use with one count + newest-`updatedAt` aggregate like History's cache, so a Mongoose write shows at once (a raw driver write wouldn't); `withLocations` stands in for `populate('location')`, and `livePacks` reads it when the fields asked for are in the catalog. The objects are shared between requests: never change them. `clearTestDB` clears it. On the profile, the page shows when `/progress` is in and the achievements strip holds two rows of grey tiles until `/achievements` arrives.
