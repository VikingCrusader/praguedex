# Card packs (卡包)

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-14 (size limit). The full list of packs, their boundaries, rules, payouts and how they show on Explore, the Map, the profile and History.

**Card packs (卡包, 2026-11-09, user's design; so far Prague Castle, Vyšehrad, Pankrác, Letná, Old Town Square, Petřín, and since 2026-11-10 Wenceslas Square, Republic Square, Na Příkopě, Kampa, Malostranské Square (new, the square had no card) and the Prokopské Valley (from Hlubočepy up the Dalejské Valley to Řeporyje), then the two street packs Evropská (`evropska-trida`, the buildings on the avenue from the west side of Vítězné náměstí to Vokovice; that slug's old card had been rewritten as the CUBE and was renamed `cube-vokovice`) and Plzeňská (`plzenska-ulice`, Anděl to Motol plus Sacré Coeur Park; the street's card was deleted), and Dejvice (`dejvice`, Antonín Engel's planned 1920s Dejvice: Vítězné náměstí, the avenues off it and the technical campus, not Baba, Hanspaulka or Šárka; the square keeps its own card, and the General Staff building and memorial are shared with Evropská), and Karlín (`karlin`, the katastrální území without Rohan Island and the Florenc tip, 17 cards since 2026-11-13: the ten Rohan cards moved to the Rohan and Libeň Islands pack on 2026-11-12 and Těšnov Park, the Hilton, Diamond Point and the Florenc card to the Florenc pack on 2026-11-13, user's calls; the slug was held by the Futurama Business Park card, renamed `futurama-business-park`), and on 2026-11-12 Holešovice (`holesovice`, only the old industrial part, 25 cards since 2026-11-13, when Štvanice Island, Tokovo, the Masaryk Schools and Maribor House joined: factories, slaughterhouse halls, port, power plant, and what artists and offices made of them) and Bubny (`bubny`, the old village and rail yard around Praha-Bubny station, 12 cards); Letná and Bubny lie in the Holešovice cadastral area but are not in the Holešovice pack, and the texts say so, and the Rohan and Libeň Islands (`rohan-and-liben-islands`: everything north of Rohanské nábřeží, which runs on the filled-in river arm, so River City counts as the island, plus the new cards `rohansky-ostrov`, `celnice-severozapadni-drahy` and `hostinec-u-budyho`, and the Libeň harbour; the Rohan Island cards are in the Karlín pack too), and Florenc (`florenc`, 2026-11-13: both sides of the flyover, from Masaryk Station and Na Florenci to the Těšnov embankment with its two ministries and Karlín's western tip; took over the one-point Florenc card, which had no check-ins, and a new card for the metro station and its clock, `stanice-metra-florenc`, stands at its spot; the Musical Theatre and the Negrelli Viaduct stay in Karlín), and on 2026-11-14 Kunratice (`kunratice`, a new slug: the old village with chateau, church and legionnaire memorial, Šeberák pond and Kunratice Forest with Nový hrad, the forest lying in Chodov's cadastral area) and Zbraslav (`zbraslav`, only the historic town: monastery and château, St James, the square, Havlín with St Gall and Charles's Pavilion, the villas, the Peace Race Bridge and the new card `praha-zbraslav-nadrazi` across the river; the South Pole of Prague, the quarry and the chapel further south are left out, user's call; it took over the one-point Zbraslav card, which had one check-in), and four more the same day: Podolí (`podoli`, along the river from the maternity hospital to the Yellow Baths plus Kavčí hory above), Barrandov (`barrandov`, the hill: rocks, terraces, studios, the estate and its tram; not Zlíchov, old Hlubočepy, the Barrandov Bridge or the Hippo Bath), Vršovice (`vrsovice`, only the old town round Vršovické náměstí and náměstí Svatopluka Čecha with Ďolíček, KOH-I-NOOR and the station, not the Eden quarter, Míčánky or the depot; it took over the one-point card `vrovice-1784036943824` and its cover) and Jižní Město (`jizni-mesto`, Háje and Chodov to the Milíčov woods; the Háje card stays a card), and on 2026-11-16 Poříčí (`porici`, the vanished riverside settlement between Revoluční and Těšnov, from both sides of Na Poříčí up to the river; the two embankment ministries, Hotel Opera and the City of Prague Museum are shared with Florenc; new slug), and Charles Square (`karlovo-namesti`, 2026-11-16: the old Cattle Market from the New Town Hall to the Faust House, the hospital and the old criminal court at its edges, Resslova to Cyril and Methodius and St Wenceslas at Zderaz, St John on the Rock; not Emmaus, user's call; took over the one-point card's slug and cover), and Národní třída (`narodni-trida`, 2026-11-11: both sides of the avenue from the National Theatre to the Adria Palace; it took over the street's card, and Café Slavia and Café Louvre became `palac-lazanskych` and `palac-louvre`, palace cards with the café in the opening), each of the others taking over its one-point card, slug and cover; Na Příkopě shares its two ends (Můstek, Koruna, Euro Palace, the Powder Tower) with the square packs, and Kampa got the new card `werichova-vila`; Castaway counts any Kampa card for the island, Wild Prague any card of the Prokopské Valley pack (`PROKOP_VALLEY_SLUGS`, read from `packs.js`)):** the cards of one connected, walkable area. **A pack must be geographically connected and easy to walk:** a merely cultural or historical grouping is not a pack (it may become a "theme" later). Static config in `server/src/data/packs.js` (hand-picked `members`, a new card doesn't join by itself; a card may be in several packs), texts in `data/packDescriptions.js` (house style of a Location description). No rarity, no coordinates; hand-picked ordinary labels; districts and quarters come from the cards. Collecting `PACK_UNLOCK_AT` (3) unlocks it (`PACK_UNLOCK_XP` 40, the full text; before that the server sends only `storyTeaser`); all of them pays `packCompleteXp` (25 per card, 100 to 800). Stages are stored on `User.packs`, pay once and stay, even if a card joins later (the progress then shows 19/20). Paid in `recordCheckIn` (`awardPacks`) and settled on `GET /api/auth/me` (`syncPacks`), announced via `newPacks` by `PackUnlocked.jsx` (tear, fan of cards, XP; achievements from the same response wait for it). `GET /api/packs`, `GET /api/packs/:slug`; `GET /api/locations/:slug` lists a card's `packs`, shown between the area pills and the story in its detail (`PackLinks`, moved there 2026-11-10, user's call, in `LocationDetail` and the Map's `SidebarDetail`; opened from a pack, that pack's button leads back to it). On the Map, a chosen card's bottom bar goes packs, then related achievements, then uncollected places nearby (user's call). Frontend: `components/packs/` (`PackCard` is the foil wrapper, built like a `LocationCard` with its classes so the two line up on History, always a closed packet except in its own detail (`torn` in `PackDetail`, 2026-11-13, user's design: an unlocked pack has its top seal torn off and three cards standing in it in a slight fan (±8°, the middle one in front and a little higher), their backs while in progress and, once complete, the faces of its three highest-XP cards (the highest in the middle) risen higher (stepped one behind the other, side by side, and one stack showing its thickness were tried and rejected the same day); a sealed pack stays sealed, and the showcase keeps the closed packet), its wrapper details (ribbed seals, side pinches, crumpled foil, gold frame round the art) kept to the empty space round the content so nothing moves, the same at both ends: grey and locked under 3 cards, in colour after, a moving holographic gradient and a glow only when complete, `styles/packs.css`; an unlocked pack opens `CardShowcase` (`pack` prop, gestures shared with cards in `hooks/useHoldGestures.js`) and turns over to `PackBack`: its foil, a centre seam, the emblem `public/pixel-art/pack-emblem.webp` and "Praguedex" in blackletter in every language, nothing else (gold on transparent, so it suits every foil colour). Thickness: on the page a darker copy of the outline (`--extrude`, no layout effect); in the showcase a real 3D solid, faces apart by `--pack-depth` with `.pack-side` walls; `PackDetail`), **on Explore packs stand among the cards (2026-11-10, user's call; `gridItems` in `LocationGrid`)**: search, labels (`labels` in `packs.js`, hand-picked for the pack as a whole; shown as clickable pills in `PackDetail`) and the area filter (any quarter or district its cards are in) find them, a pack within 100 m (`NEARBY_REVEAL_M`) of any of its cards goes first whatever the sort, nearest first, complete ones too (left out for a few hours on 2026-11-16, then put back once the phone banner made them cost no room), right behind the locked cards within 100 m, which lead (2026-11-16, user's call; without the side column, i.e. on phones, those packs leave the grid for a one-line banner above it, `.near-packs`, `nearPacksAsBanner` on `LocationGrid`), the rest stand at their nearest card when sorting by distance; sorted by rarity, every pack goes to the bottom, after common (packs have no rarity; user's call 2026-11-11); sorted by Newest, packs stand among the cards by `released` (each pack's go-live time in `packs.js`, newest first; `packsByRelease`); only a guest with no position gets the hand-picked spread, a pack at every 5th place, best known first (`interleavePacks`, `FEATURED_PACK_SLUGS` in `utils/gridOrder.js`), while a signed-in player or anyone with a position gets each pack just before the first of its own cards (`packsAtTheirCards`; 2026-11-11, user's call: famous packs used to jump the queue under Newest), and without a position the first page of cards is the hand-picked `FEATURED_SLUGS` (48 of Prague's best-known places, every rarity among them, four to each gap between packs, so the 12 packs and 48 cards fill the first 60), the rest taking the rarities in turn (`diverseOrder`); a first visit decides whether someone signs up, so no obscure cards on that page and no wall of sealed packs (2026-11-10, user's call); they used to be shown uncovered to a guest with no position (name, cover and frame in full, a small corner lock), but since 2026-11-14 a guest's wall is all locked like everyone's (user's call: it feels more like a dex and makes people want to collect, and the Guide and the welcome board already show what a collected card looks like); `PackCard` keeps `uncovered` for the Guide's packs demo; after a search, a label or an area the matching packs lead instead; My Collections, the treasure filter and a rarity filter show cards only. The Packs button shows only packs (rarity and sort off; labels, search and area still apply). Covers are the normal manifest by slug: large packs that show nearly every place from a precise prompt, small or landscape ones from a photo (user's call). **A pack's slug can stand wherever a card's slug goes in `relatedLandmarks` and wins over a card of the same slug**, and in `AREA_LANDMARKS`. **History rule (user's call, 2026-11-09): every event in which a pack's area is the subject or the main stage links that pack, first in `relatedLandmarks` (the Castle before Vyšehrad when both are there); an event that merely names the place in passing doesn't.** Background cards still never get landmarks. In `PackDetail` the pack's quarters and districts are the same clickable pills as a card's (`PackAreaLine` in `DistrictLine.jsx`), one per area its cards are in, most cards first. **On the Map (2026-11-10, user's call):** a Packs button in the top bar lists the packs the filters leave in the right column and narrows the map to their cards (rarity off, as on Explore); a pack from that list or the bottom bar opens in the right column (`PackPanel` in `PackDetail.jsx`, the same body as the pop-up; a phone uses a sheet), a card clicked in it lies on top and closing the card comes back to the pack; with an area chosen, its packs lead the bottom bar. An open pack is a filter of its own: the map shows only its cards (area and scope step aside; labels, rarity and search still narrow it outside the Packs filter), it leads the filter chips (its ✕ closes it), and the map fits its cards (`pack` on `MapAreaFocus`, at most zoom 16), going back to the area or the city when it closes. **On the Map and the profile a pack shows only its art, foil and seals (`artOnly` on `PackCard`, 2026-11-10, user's call)**: no name, bar or count on the packet, also at the top of its detail there (on the profile the name and progress stand in words under it, `PackCaption`, with an "All" link top right like the other strips); the showcase still picks up the whole packet, and Explore and History keep the full pack, as does the Map's Packs list on every screen (user's call, 2026-11-10: two to a row where each gets 155px, one in a Mac's narrower column); only a pack opened in the Map's right column shows art only.
