# Location cards: content rules

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-20 (verbatim). Read before adding, editing, deleting or relabelling any Location card.

## Adding new locations (`server/src/data/seedLocations.js`)

- Every entry needs a full `description: { en, cz, zh }` — all three languages, not just `en`.
- English description structure: an opening paragraph of ~50 words that introduces the location simply and humorously, followed by one or two main-text paragraphs with the substantive detail, followed by a final Bonus paragraph. CZ and ZH mirror this structure.
- Do not include Czech-name/place-name translations anywhere in the EN or ZH description text — in particular, no parenthetical Czech-translation note in the EN/ZH opening paragraph (e.g. no `Welcome to X (Český název)!` pattern).
- **History Timeline and Location descriptions are two separate content types — don't blur them (confirmed 2026-08-29).** A Location's `description` should not reference the History Timeline as a shared narrative the reader has supposedly already progressed through — no `"the Hussite general this project has already met never losing a single battle"`-style phrasing (`kost-castle`'s original draft), no `"this timeline has already told"` callbacks, no assuming the reader has read a specific History Timeline event. A Location card is meant to stand on its own; if a figure or event also appears on the History Timeline, that's a coincidence of subject matter, not a excuse to write as if the two features share one continuous story. (Some earlier entries — `bezdez-castle`, `zricenina-hradu-rotstejn`, `liberec` — already have this kind of cross-reference baked in from before this rule was confirmed; not retroactively fixed unless the user asks, but don't add more.)
- Don't add a `landmark` label to any new entry.
- `rarity` (city cards only; an `outside` card becomes `expedition` automatically, whatever the seed entry says): `legend` is reserved for genuinely monumental/grand/massive buildings — usable for a new entry, not just the original preset landmarks, but sparingly. **Scope update (2026-08-22):** the original "never `legend` for new entries" rule was set when new locations were still Prague-only, where few buildings left to add were actually grand enough — now that new locations span all of Bohemia, a sufficiently monumental building anywhere in Bohemia (e.g. `zamek-hluboka-nad-vltavou`) can justify `legend` too. Default to `common`/`rare`/`superior`/`epic`/`mythic` for everything else. `xpReward` stays in sync automatically via `RARITY_XP` in `data/rarityMap.js`.
- `coordinates` are supplied by the user for each new location — don't look them up.
- **City boundary and district (2026-10-11):** a location outside Prague's administrative boundary (OSM relation 435514) gets the label `outside` (布拉格以外), appended last so the primary label stays first; Explore and the Map sidebar hide those cards by default ("City only" toggle, `ScopeToggle.jsx`). A location inside the boundary instead gets `district: 'Praha N'`, its správní obvod (1–22: each Praha N městská část plus its smaller neighbours, per cs.wikipedia "Členění Prahy"), computed by point-in-polygon against the 57 OSM městská část boundaries (admin_level 9), and `quarter`, its katastrální území (112 of them, the historical towns/villages: `'Staré Město'`, `'Smíchov'`, … — official Czech name; EN/ZH display names in `client/src/utils/pragueQuarters.js`). Both come from ČÚZK's RÚIAN service (`ags.cuzk.gov.cz/arcgis/rest/services/RUIAN/Prohlizeci_sluzba_nad_daty_RUIAN/MapServer`, layer 7 KatastralniUzemi with `obec=554782`, layer 10 SpravniObvodVHlavnimMestePraze), which agreed 100% with the OSM-based districts. `DistrictLine.jsx` shows quarter + district (or "Outside Prague") as their own pill row under the descriptive labels in both detail views. The four Prague poles stay inside, no `outside` label.
- **Expedition cards must carry weight (user's rule, 2026-11-15):** an `outside` card has to be worth a trip on its own, a place people travel out of Prague specifically to see. Minor spots (a research lab, a cottage, a valley, a viaduct, a hill, a trail junction, a pond, a village, a swimming pool, places that only matter in a video game) don't get a card; on 2026-11-15 fourteen such cards were deleted (HiLASE, Staročeská chalupa, Ohrobecké údolí, Žampašský most, U Tří Habrů, Dachova pool and the eight Kingdom Come cards around Trosky other than the castle; Pahorek Krliš was deleted too and restored the same day, since `seven-legendary-dukes` links it: check History links before deleting any card). The bar rises with distance (user's calls, same day): near Prague, a village, small town, chapel or pond with distinctive scenery or some special meaning is fine as an entry-level expedition (expeditions are about novelty, and the near ones are where players start); farther out a place needs real historical weight to be worth the trip. The nearer a card, the lower its priority for deletion, except a place that looks like the city and is plain dull, which goes first. Cards linked from the History Timeline stay. Lidice and Kutná Hora keep all their cards. A second pass the same day deleted Letohrádek Belvedér, Zámek Horoměřice, Kamínek, Zámek Dolní Břežany, the Gloriet tower and Hostivické rybníky, and merged the Pernštýnské Square card into `zamek-pardubice` (297 m apart; its old-town paragraph is now the castle card's second main paragraph). When the user proposes a card, say if it seems too slight. The `EXPEDITION_XP_OVERRIDES` "down" list exists for small cards bundled with a bigger one, not as a way to keep slight cards.
- **Scope rule for non-Prague locations (confirmed 2026-08-23): this app is Prague-centred (named "Prague Stories" until 2026-11-14, now "Praguedex") — a city outside Prague only ever gets *one* Location card, treated as a single day-trip destination, not a second city to build out landmark-by-landmark.** Don't create a separate card for every notable sight within that same non-Prague city (e.g. a second statue, a second church) — fold anything else worth mentioning into that one city card's own description instead (as a sentence in its tourism paragraph, the way `ceske-budejovice`'s founder statue is handled) or into the History Timeline event's own prose, not as its own `relatedLandmarks` entry. The one standing exception is an out-of-town castle/monastery some distance from its nearest town (e.g. `bezdez-castle`, `zamek-hluboka-nad-vltavou`) — those can still get their own card even when a nearby town already has one. **Locations outside the Czech Republic never get a card at all** (e.g. Marchegg, Austria) — mention them only in running prose, never as a Location. **Second exception, confirmed 2026-08-24 (`vlassky-dvur`, `kostel-svate-barbory`, `kostnice-sedlec`, all in/around Kutná Hora):** the user's own stated two-part test for this specific exception — "库特纳霍拉是例外，因为离布拉格够近+旅游景点够硬，我才拆出来的" — a town has to clear **both** bars: (1) genuinely close enough to Prague to function as a satellite/day-trip town, not a distant second city, **and** (2) the individual sub-landmark itself has to be a serious, independently-major attraction in its own right (a real museum/monument people specifically travel to see — Kutná Hora's mint, cathedral, and ossuary all separately draw visitors), not just any notable building inside an already-carded town. Both conditions together, not either alone. This is a judgment call, not a blanket reopening of the one-card rule — flag it to the user rather than assuming it applies elsewhere, even for other Prague-adjacent towns (see `kutna-hora`'s own card, which still folds St. Barbara's Cathedral's basic facts into its own description even after St. Barbara's got a separate card, since the two serve different purposes: the town card's quick mention vs. the landmark card's full depth).
- **Third exception, confirmed 2026-08-24 (`olomouc`, and `brno`/`slavkov-u-brna` flagged for when they come up): a city can earn its own card purely on "just barely a Prague day trip + outsized standalone historical weight" grounds, without needing a Kutná Hora-style sub-landmark split.** User's own reasoning for Olomouc: close enough to still nominally count as a day trip from Prague, and it's the site of Wenceslas III's 1306 assassination (a major History Timeline event) plus its own separate deep bench of later history (Thirty Years' War Swedish occupation, the 1758 Prussian siege, 1848 imperial abdication). Brno was named in the same breath as a second, not-yet-built example, with Slavkov u Brna (Austerlitz, ~200km from Prague — well past the usual distance norms) flagged as a further one to revisit once the Napoleonic era is reached. **The house style for this exception is the opposite of the Kutná Hora pattern: write ONE long, rich card for the city itself (the `plzen`/`jihlava` template — intro paragraph, historical development, tourist landmarks folded into the same card's prose, closing bonus) rather than splitting named sub-landmarks into their own separate Location entries.** `olomouc`'s own card folds in its Holy Trinity Column, St. Wenceslas Cathedral, the Přemyslid bishop's palace, and its astronomical clock this way — none of those got (or should get) their own card.
- After writing the new entries, run `node src/data/seedLocations.js` (from `server/`) to upsert them into the database — just run it once the content is done, no need to ask first.

## Labels

**Labels:** The primary label is always first in the array (used for AI description generation fallback). Label names, emoji and colours live in `LABEL_DEFINITIONS` / `LABEL_COLORS` in `utils/pixelArtMap.js` (the filter, pills and editors all read from there; the `label.*` keys in `LanguageContext`'s `T` object are leftovers nothing reads). **Era and style labels (2026-10-15)** replaced the catch-all `historical`, `architecture` and `modern` (and `basilica`, merged into `church`); they are ordinary labels, but the filter panel (Explore and Map sidebar, shared `LabelFilterGroups.jsx`) shows all labels under group headings from `LABEL_GROUPS` in `pixelArtMap.js`: Era, Style, Buildings, Places, Other (user's request; a test checks every label is in exactly one group, so add new labels to a group). Eras: `prehistory` (and legends, to ~900), `medieval` (~900–1526), `early-modern` (1526–1790), `national-revival` (1790–1918), `world-wars` (1914–1945), `communism`; no post-1989 era. Styles: `romanesque`, `gothic`, `renaissance`, `baroque` (incl. Rococo), `classicism` (incl. Empire), `historicism` (all neo-styles: never tag a neo-Gothic building `gothic`), `art-nouveau`, `cubism` (incl. Rondocubism), `functionalism` (the Czech term, not "Bauhaus"), `brutalism`, `contemporary`. Tagging rule: a building gets its 1–2 dominant visible styles and no era, unless its story centres on another era (e.g. Cyril and Methodius cathedral: `baroque` + `world-wars`); a non-building (monument, site, square, battlefield) gets an era; nature/cafés may get neither. A label with fewer than 5 cards is not created (user's rule; socialist realism was dropped for that reason). **Monument vs sculpture (split 2026-10-31, user's call):** `monument` (纪念碑) is for what was put up to remember a person, event or victims (memorials, obelisks, crosses, mounds, sites); `sculpture` (雕塑) for a card about a sculpture as a work of art (David Černý's pieces, fountains with figures, the Charles Bridge saints); a commemorative statue of a person (Hus, Žižka, Charles IV, St Wenceslas) and the plague/Marian columns get both. Squares, parks and ordinary cemeteries don't get `monument` just because something stands in them. The History Fan achievement counts check-ins on cards with any era/style label (`HISTORY_LABELS` in `gamification.js`, `historyCount` in the check-in stats).
