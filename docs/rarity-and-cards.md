# Rarity, expedition, secret cards and card design

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-20 (verbatim). Read before touching rarity, XP, expedition/secret rules, card materials, frames or card animation.

## Rarity and XP (backend)

- Rarity and XP are coupled: `RARITY_XP` in `data/rarityMap.js` is the single source of truth for the six city tiers. A `pre('validate')` hook on `Location` (`resolveRarity`) keeps `rarity`/`xpReward` in step with labels and coordinates on every save, and `seedLocations.js` applies the same function before its upsert.
- **Expedition (远征 / Výprava, 2026-10-16):** every `outside` card has the seventh rarity `expedition`, never a city tier (a card that loses `outside` drops to `common`). Its XP comes from the straight-line distance to Charles Bridge: under 25 km 60, 25–50 km 120, 50–100 km 180, 100–150 km 240, 150 km and over 300 (`EXPEDITION_XP_STEPS`), never below 60. `EXPEDITION_XP_OVERRIDES` moves cards up (famous/grand: Karlštejn 240, Krumlov and Hluboká 300, …) or down (small sights bundled with a bigger nearby card: the Trosky/KCD ponds and villages, Tetín's small cards, two Kutná Hora cards, …); all user-approved, and only one of the five steps is allowed. After changing either table, run `npm run sync:expedition`. Applies to check-ins from 2026-10-16 on; past check-ins keep the XP they paid, and level thresholds were deliberately left unchanged. Frontend (materials 2026-11-16, user's calls): 60 is brushed silver with tarnished edges, 120 honey amber in an ivory frame set with pearls, lettered dark brown (platinum until the same day; amber so the three steps differ, and kept mid-tone so it never reads as legend's dark gold; silver and rubies were tried first on the amber, but red on orange was dull), with the drifting glow in deep red and under the cover, 180 and up dark platinum (steel blue-grey, mirror-bright frame, a wide glow drifting slowly across it, `.loc-card__fx--band`, 20 s), which also adds its fuller corners, the glint, the twinkling stones and silver sparks on the sheet (`RarityFx.jsx`); 120's pearl doesn't shine (`pixelart.css`, `data-rarity="expedition"`), with the stone in the four edge settings picked from `xpReward` (`expeditionGem` in `utils/rarity.js`, `data-gem` on `LocationCard`): 60 bronze rivet, 120 pearl (the key was `ruby` until 2026-11-16), 180 and up a deep-blue diamond (the only animated one, and its frame is fancier: mythic's fuller corner flourish, a silver glint round the ornaments, a blue outer glow; obsidian and emerald for 120/180 and ruby for 240 were the first design, changed the same day at the user's call so more cards carry diamonds). The card face just says 远征 / Expedition / Výprava like any tier. The rarity filter on Explore and Map only offers Expedition with "Show all" (and drops it when switching back to City only). **The rarity filter is multi-select (since 2026-11-08; single-select from 2026-10-16)** on Explore and Map: each pick adds to the set, picking one again takes it off. On Explore with exactly one rarity picked, its one-line description shows under the title / "where you are" line (`.explore-rarity-blurb`); that slot shows one thing at a time, rarity description first, then the area blurb, then the My Collections hint. While a rarity is picked, the intro line (city / Show all blurb) is not shown either; the "where you are" badge stays. The descriptions live in `utils/rarityBlurbs.js` (`RARITY_BLURB`), which the Guide's rarity list is also built from, so edit them there, not in `GuidePage.jsx`.
- **Secret cards (秘藏 / Secret / Tajné, 2026-11-15, user's design):** outside cards that are not famous or grand in themselves and matter mostly for one History Timeline event, or lie outside Bohemia (Moravia), get rarity `secret` (`SECRET_SLUGS` in `data/rarityMap.js`; `resolveRarity` sets it, run `npm run sync:expedition` after changing the list). They are found only as related landmarks on the History Timeline: Explore, the Map, search, filters and the Guide's demos drop an uncollected one (`isFindable` in `client/src/utils/rarity.js`, applied on Explore and the Map through `useFindable`, which keeps the whole list and filters by position; since 2026-11-16, user's call, an uncollected one shows there while the player is within 8 km of a `city` card or 3 km of any other, and goes again when they leave, nothing remembered; the one exception is the Rarity window's example, Sion's ruins, `SECRET_SAMPLE_SLUG` in `GuideBoard.jsx`, user's pick 2026-11-16, shown as the eighth card in two rows of four, with its line in `RARITY_BLURB`; picked, it shows a button to its History event, `SECRET_SAMPLE_EVENT`), and the profile's totals leave them out until collected (`secretTotal` in `locationCatalog.js`). Once the player has one, Secret appears as an eighth rarity in the profile's and the collection's rarity counts (`rarity-compact__list--eight`) and in the Explore and Map rarity filters with Show all, listing only their own secret cards (`withSecret`, `hasSecretCard` in `utils/rarity.js`; user's call, 2026-11-15). The proximity alerts still know them all, so they collect on the spot like any card, and a collected one shows everywhere. XP is the expedition step raised (`SECRET_XP`: 60→90, 120→150, 180→270, 240→360, 300→500). Distance doesn't matter (user's rule, 2026-11-16, replacing an earlier "nothing within 50 km"): any card with little weight of its own that is firmly tied to a History event can be secret, near Prague too; Lidice and Kutná Hora still never are. Near ones so far: Tetín's cross and St John of Nepomuk church (its castle ruins, required by `ludmilas_veil`, and St Ludmila's church stay visible), Pahorek Krliš, the Pičhora cremation cemetery (44 km, linked from the timeline's first event, so a reader meets a secret card on page one), Loděnice, Lázně Toušeň and Lobkovice Chateau. Stříbrná Skalice stays visible (Kingdom Come fans travel there; user's call). An achievement that lists places shows a secret card in its detail; check before making one secret and ask the user (`socha-premysla-otakara-ii` is secret and still one of `iron_and_golden_king`'s towns, user's call: a clue there is fine). A test checks every secret slug is linked from a History event. First 12 (2026-11-15): Jihlava, Veveří, Znojmo, Brno, Olomouc, Vladař, Krasíkov, Přimda, Ovčí vrch, Stříbro, Tachov, Husinec; then Žižkova mohyla and Chlumec, then Příběnice, the Valdice charterhouse and Březnice, then Havlíčkův Brod, Rokycany, the Libice hillfort and the statue beside it (hidden with it so it doesn't point the way; it pays 150 through a 120 override, below the hillfort), then Vrch Gothard, Lemberk, Malešov and Sion (near Kutná Hora, but not its cards), then Svojanov and the Otakar II statue in Vysoké Mýto. ## Rarity (frontend)

**Rarity:** Six city tiers: `common / rare / superior / epic / mythic / legend`. XP: 10 / 20 / 30 / 50 / 70 / 100. The `superior` tier colour is `#2c8c03`. Outside cards are `expedition` (60–300 XP by distance, see **Expedition** above).

## Off-screen cards pause

**Off-screen cards pause (2026-11-09):** one shared `IntersectionObserver` (`utils/pauseOffscreen.js`, used by every `LocationCard` except the showcase copy) sets `data-offscreen`, and `pixelart.css` pauses that card's animations, except a card mid-flip, whose animation end clears its state. It is an attribute, not a class, so React never overwrites it. A locked legend card used to keep a rotating border light (user's call, 2026-11-09); since 2026-11-14, when legend's frame light was dropped, no locked card moves. A card animations switch in the profile's settings window (`components/dashboard/SettingsWindow.jsx`, which now holds only 屏幕常亮 / keep screen on) existed 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-15; it was removed once the card effects moved to the GPU (user's call), with `utils/cardMotion.js` and every `data-card-motion` rule.

## Card motion runs on the GPU

**Card motion runs on the GPU (2026-11-14, user: kept, it cured the stutter):** every looping effect on a card is its own element (`RarityFx.jsx`, classes `loc-card__fx--*`; legend's embers sit in the card body; epic's label glow is `.loc-card__labels::after`) and animates only `transform` or `opacity`, so nothing repaints per frame (a screen of legend cards took 4.4 s of main-thread time in 5 s before, 0.4 s after). Keep to it: never animate a background, shadow, filter or registered custom property on a card; a moving piece that needs a shape moves inside a still parent that holds the mask; the elements are `display: none` off screen, on locked cards (a locked legend keeps the light on its ring), and under reduced motion. The glint on the diamond step's ornaments is one masked overlay with a turning wedge of light; its masks (`--gm-*` in `pixelart.css`) are the `--orn` drawings turned for each corner and edge, so redraw them when an ornament changes.

## Card paper and frames

**Card paper (2026-11-14, user's design, worked out card by card):** the six city tiers' materials, as the Guide names them (2026-11-15): paper, porcelain, jade, amethyst (plain deep violet, no texture, under one faint static step cut, three chamfered rectangles lit from the top left, and three tiny glints), Bohemian garnet, gold. a collected card is printed on its rarity's own material, the same in both themes, so it reads as a solid object instead of a frame over the page; a locked card shows its frame in grey (`filter: grayscale(1)`) with no glows and no motion, so a first visit already looks rich, and collecting it brings the colour (2026-11-15, user's call; until then a locked card kept the theme's plain surface and showed its frame only on hover). Since 2026-11-16 (user's call) every locked card is printed on one mid-dark grey sheet (`--panel: #3b3e45`, light lettering, one light grey frame colour, legend's frame dimmed), since the materials in grey made a light-and-dark patchwork; the frame's shape alone tells the tier. The whole card is one sheet: the cover sits straight on it (no label-coloured mat; the label pill is the one place the label colour shows) with a keyline, and the text is in a panel with a double rule. Common cream paper (plus a floret pattern and a diamond rule over the rarity), rare pale blue porcelain (a diamond-and-dots rule; deepened to `#bcd5ec` and turned from paper into a grainless glaze with a static gloss, a faint diagonal highlight and deeper edges, 2026-11-15, user's call: above paper, below jade), superior jade with a glow in the rarity's corner (deepened from pale `#b8d8c0` to mid `#8fc29d` 2026-11-15 to bridge the step to epic; still dark text), epic lavender raw silk with a slowly breathing deep purple glow round the label, mythic plain deep wine red with no texture (user: the glow alone looks best), lettered in light rose, its only motion the stone's glow breathing (two clouds of rose light under the cover fading in and out of step; it replaced a drifting band) (light red leather, oxblood leather and a garnet texture came first; user wanted legend's dark lacquer look, a step quieter), legend gilded lacquer lettered in gold, the darkest card and the only gold one (pale gold leaf was rejected: the gold frame vanished on it), expedition brushed silver, honey amber in ivory (120) or dark platinum (180 and up) with its stones. Each tier sets `--tex`, `--panel`, `--ink`, `--ink-soft`, `--ink-rc` and friends in the "Card paper" block of `pixelart.css`, which documents them; the tiles are built from grayscale images in `client/brand-src/texture-*.png` (generated by the user from prompts that ask for seamless, grayscale, low-contrast, flat-lit, no-line textures) by `node scripts/buildCardTextures.mjs` (client/) into `public/brand/textures/`. Lessons: overlay-blending a grey tile flattens it on a pale paper and hard-light turns its darks to grey dirt, so pale materials are baked in colour (`tint`/`base` in the script, `base` must equal the tier's `--panel`); patterns and busy textures (printed flowers, linen, marble veins, raw silk slubs at full strength) read as clutter, the user wants material and frame, not ornament. **Frames (same day):** the rule over the rarity grows fancier tier by tier (`--rule-mark`, `--rule-alpha`); rare has the double line with a pair of small scrolls in each corner (superior's until 2026-11-15; a single line with corner squares in between looked plain); superior has its own rounded botanical ornament, a four-petal flower with a laurel sprig of round leaves and a berry along each arm and the same flower between two leaves mid top and bottom, in its deep green (2026-11-15, user's call; a jade-ring frame was rejected earlier, and neither tier keeps a corner line of its own); mythic's ornaments are Gothic (quarter rose windows, hanging cusps, quatrefoils) so it no longer looks like legend; epic's corners are the user's own reference ornament, keyed from a picture into a mask by `node scripts/buildOrnaments.mjs` (client/; source in `brand-src/ornament-*.png`, output `public/brand/ornaments/`) after Art Nouveau and Romanesque tries and three hand-drawn SVG copies failed. Legend's corners are an acanthus L the user generated from a prompt (`legend-corner.png`, its lines thickened before keying), 40% of the card wide, joined by a straight CSS-drawn gilt band on the four edge ornaments (a main line between two hairlines, lozenges and beads alternating; a waving vine came first and read as untidy, 2026-11-15) (the card is a size container, legend only, so they are placed in `cqw`); no gems (stones are the expedition's alone; a glowing jewel in the top and bottom bands was tried and dropped 2026-11-15); its foil sweep, the light circling its frame, a drifting band and a passing gleam were all dropped as glaring or busy: its motion is now three clouds of light in three golds (amber, rose gold, champagne) breathing and wandering under the cover on cycles that never line up, so the lacquer shifts in colour (a step above mythic's two one-colour clouds), plus the embers. Every card's text sits 12px higher (rarity line and distance pushed back down near the bottom, legend's kept up as the exception, card height unchanged); the text panel's double rule is gone on every card; epic, mythic and legend are the three dark cards (epic deep amethyst, plain since 2026-11-15), each with a breathing glow round its label in its own colour, and a collected legend gives off a soft gold glow. When the user shows a reference ornament, key it rather than redraw it, and never key a watermarked stock image (a Shutterstock preview was declined; the user generated an original instead). Invented ornaments were mostly rejected: show one tier, wait for a yes.
