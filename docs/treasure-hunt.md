# Treasure Hunt (寻宝)

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-14 (size limit). The full design, rules, clue order, multiplier and page layouts.

**Treasure Hunt (寻宝):** the only game on the `/treasure-hunt` page (`TreasureHuntPage.jsx`, `TreasureHuntPanel.jsx`; old `/random-draw` redirects there). **One hunt since 2026-10-29 (user's call):** the separate Clues and Hardcore modes (2026-10-12 / 2026-10-15) were merged, keeping what was tense in each and dropping what was dull or frustrating. Core rule: **walking earns clues for free, pressing the button and buying clues cost multiplier.** API routes and `User.randomDraw` keep their old "random draw" names (from the open draw, 明抽, removed 2026-10-12 as boring; a leftover `mode: 'open'` draw counts as no draw and pays no bonus). One hunt per 24h, login only (guests see the rules and a disabled start button). No re-roll (dropped in the merge), no time bonus (a 2-hour x1.5 was removed 2026-10-12 so a hard hunt doesn't feel rushed). Pool (`huntPoolMatch`): uncollected city cards (not `outside`) with a quarter, minus `HUNT_EXCLUDED_SLUGS` in `gamification.js` (bridges over the Vltava, Charles Bridge statues, the islands and what stands on them, the Old Town Weir, the Podbaba ferry: the river-side clue can't describe them, user's call 2026-10-15). The treasure is picked at least 4 km (`HUNT_MIN_START_M`) from where the player starts the hunt, so every hunt is a walk; with nothing that far left, any pool card will do (user's call, 2026-10-29). **A hunt can only be started inside Prague (2026-10-30, user's call):** `drawRandomLocation` needs a position (400 `positionRequired`) inside the city boundary (403 `outsidePrague`), checked by `utils/pragueBoundary.js` against `data/pragueBoundary.json` (the 22 správní obvody, written by `client/scripts/buildPragueAreas.mjs` next to the client's own copy); skipped in development like the check-in distance check; the page shows the reason under the start button. Location and coordinates never go to the client until found; the server computes every clue (`buildHuntClues` in `userController.js`) from the position the client reports (`GET /api/user/random-draw?lat&lng`, polled every 10 s), and that call also stores the clues walking brought.

- **Clues (reworked 2026-10-31, user's call, for the thrill of a treasure slowly giving itself away; replaces the 2026-10-29/30 designs with planned, pre-checked label clues and random bought clues).** Nothing but the distance at the start. **Why attribute clues come late:** labels, rarity, quarter and district are filters on Explore and the map, where every locked card shows its labels and distance. Distance (`huntReading`): rounded to the whole km from 3 km out (5.8 and 6.4 km both "about 6 km", stated in the rules), to the half km from 3 km down to 1 km, then the bands `{ band: [500, 1000] }` "not far now", `{ band: [300, 500] }` "very close", and `{ within: 300 }` "right around you" (whole sentences in the panel). **Never "within 100 m" (the hit radius, dropped 2026-10-29):** with it a press could never miss. Hot and cold every ~100 m (`HUNT_TREND_STEP_M`, under 40 m = same) **only outside `HUNT_TREND_MAX_M` (500 m)**: inside it the clues are enough and a verdict every 100 m would point the way, which only a miss should do. **The clues come in a fixed order (`HUNT_CLUE_ORDER`): river bank (`side`, from the quarter via `LEFT_BANK_QUARTERS`: "castle side" / "Old Town side"), district, quarter, then the label or the rarity 50/50 (`HUNT_CLOSE_KEYS`; a label is any of the card's labels, each as likely, stored on the clue as `label`: the luck is deliberate, no pre-screening), then the other one.** Walking brings each for free at `HUNT_FREE_CLUE_AT` (3 km, 1 km, 500 m, 300 m; the last one is buy-only), pushed with `free: true` (doesn't count toward the multiplier), flashed once. **Buying (`POST /api/user/random-draw/clue`, `takeHuntClue`) gets the next clue in the order early, from anywhere; a clue already had is skipped when walking reaches its distance.** No bought direction any more: the direction only comes with a miss. The response carries `nextClue` (a key, or `rarityOrLabel` while that slot is undrawn) for the buy button and confirm, and `nextFree`. Hunts dealt before the rework (`firstClue`/`labelOrder` set) count the river side as had and an old `area` clue as district plus quarter (`cluesHad`); `planHuntClues`, `orderHuntLabels` and the label/quarter checks are gone. The XP figure shows only once the rarity is known.
- **Presses and multiplier.** The button works anywhere (`POST /api/user/random-draw/guess`); within 100 m (`HIDDEN_DRAW_HIT_M`) it finds the card, and every press asks for confirmation. `HUNT_MULTIPLIERS = [10, 5, 3, 2, 1]` (2026-10-31): **a miss drops two rungs (10 → 3 → 1: without clues only two presses pay a bonus), a bought clue one rung.** 1x is the card's plain XP, the floor; presses are unlimited (user's call: no zero-XP outcome). A miss reports only its direction from the pressed spot, never a distance; misses never end the hunt, so a hunt only fails by timing out, then `lastHunt` reveals the answer and later check-ins are 1x. **Only a press on the Treasure Hunt page pays the multiplier (2026-10-30, user's call):** a normal check-in on the target pays the card's plain XP and ends the hunt unfound (`randomDraw.forfeited`; presses and clues 409), since otherwise a player could check in at every nearby candidate for free. Leftover `{ skipped: true }` misses from the old clue mode count as a clue (`huntStepOf`, `huntMissCount`). The rules' clue table (`HuntClueTable` in `TreasureHuntPage.jsx`) hard-codes the clue order and free distances and must mirror the server constants. **The panel (`TreasureHuntPanel`, 2026-11-14, step one of the page's redesign):** a reward ladder (10x to 1x, current rung lit, the rung a pending miss or buy would leave marked with dashes, each rung's XP once the rarity is known) and a clue track (the five slots of the order as a path, each row with its free distance or "buy only", had rows showing their value and free/bought, a new one turning over once, a "You are here" pin after the last free distance the reading is surely inside). `clueTrack` works the slots out from the existing `hunt` fields, so the server sends nothing new; the Guide's practice hunt uses the same panel. **The page (2026-11-14, redone for every screen):** three layouts in `TreasureHuntPage.jsx`, styles in `styles/hunt.css`: `board` (landscape from 1024 x 600: three columns filling one screen, columns scroll on their own, never the page; **redone the same day, user's call: a first visit met a wall of text, and the game is hot and cold, so the card and "It's here!" take the middle and the most room**: left a four-step How to play (`draw.huntStep1-4`), a Full rules button opening `HuntRulesWindow` with the walk-through, rules and clue table, the practice link and one row of finds; middle the title, the card as big as the height allows, then the panel in `split` mode (distance, buttons, ladder); right Clues: during a hunt the panel portals its clue track and misses there (`trackTarget`), otherwise the static clue table, then the clue map and the last answer. **Redone again on 2026-11-16 (user's design), with the six-picture comic (`components/draw/HuntComic.jsx`, texts and rules in `utils/huntComic.js`, pictures `public/guide/hunt-comic/1-6.webp`, a distance tag and a caption over/under each, translated; the pictures carry only the game's own English on the phone):** the columns are now play (`hunt-col--play`: the Guide's treasure-map picture `/guide/hunt.webp` over the card, a smaller card while the picture shows, the start button, the last answer and the Full rules / practice buttons; during a hunt the picture steps aside for the panel), comic (`hunt-col--comic`: the title, the lead `draw.huntLead` and all six panels, three across; the four short steps `draw.huntStep1-4` are gone from the board) and clues (the table or the live track, the map, and before a hunt the finds, which moved here from the left; during a hunt they are left out for the track's room). On the stack and phone the comic is a strip to swipe through, at the top of How to play, and it replaced the three-paragraph walk-through (`draw.huntIntro`, kept in the strings); the Guide's Hunt window shows it too. Each piece gets its own bigger font size on a 2K screen; scaling the whole board with CSS zoom was tried and rejected, user: odd next to the navbar). **On a 2K screen nothing scrolls (user's rule, 2026-11-14):** `treasureHunt.spec.js` checks every column for overflow in all three languages, with a confirm open too, `stack` (upright tablets, short windows: one column) and `phone` (≤720 px: the stack with full-width, thumb-sized buttons in the panel, under the clues and above the map; a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen was tried and dropped the same day, user's call; the card shrinks to 200 px while hunting (150 px until 2026-11-14), the panel's buttons stand on their own between the card and the panel (the ladder, distance and clue track), outside its box (`.hunt-panel-actions`, also on the stack and in the Guide's practice hunt; 2026-11-14, user's call), How to play and the finds further down the page, no buttons under the title). on the stack and phone How to play stays on the page in full, on the board only the short version (Full rules opens the rest); no Keep screen on button (user's call: it is in the profile's settings, and `useWakeLock` on the page honours it). `HuntMap` draws the clues on Prague (the river-bank clue lights that bank's quarters, from `LEFT_BANK_QUARTERS` mirrored in `utils/huntRules.js`; the district; with the quarter it zooms into the district) plus the player's dot, worked out on the device; `HuntCompass` sums up the misses' directions; `HuntFinds` shows the finds as the profile's small covers with three figures (finds, best multiplier, first-press finds); a find plays `HuntCelebration` (rays, XP counting up); the first load shows grey shapes, no spinner; the last answer is a small card. The challenge box under the tagline is gone (2026-11-10, user's call: too long and repeating the rules); its tone moved into `draw.huntIntro` under the start button, three paragraphs (`\n\n`) walking through a hunt: what is hidden, how the clues come, how a find pays. **Design intent:** 10x should come from walking all the way there and hitting on the first press; a long walk is the point, don't tune it toward quick or easy.
- **Storage and display.** The hitting press is `CheckIn.guessAttempt` (and the XP paid `xpEarned`); old hardcore finds still carry `CheckIn.huntMode: 'hardcore'` and their detail line still says "硬核寻宝所得" (`detail.huntFindHardcore`); new finds say "寻宝所得，N次猜中 +Y XP" (`detail.huntFind`) in `LocationDetail` and the `MapPage` sidebar. Cards carry no hunt badge (removed 2026-10-13). Sounds (`utils/sound.js`, synthesized with Web Audio like the other app sounds, no audio files): `playShuffleSound` on start (timed to the shuffle animation's keyframes), `playClueSound` on a clue taken, `playMissSound` on a miss, `playHuntWinSound` on a find; after a find the XP update (and any level-up modal with its own fanfare) waits `HUNT_WIN_SOUND_MS` so the two don't overlap. Each tap handler calls `unlockAudio()` first, since iOS keeps an AudioContext started after an await silent. Starting a hunt plays `DeckShuffle.jsx` (2.3 s, CSS `deck-*` keyframes; `SHUFFLE_MS` must match; skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`). The old `User.hardcoreDraw` is out of the schema; `npm run purge:hunt-positions` (server/) unsets leftover ones along with ended hunts' `trendRef`.
